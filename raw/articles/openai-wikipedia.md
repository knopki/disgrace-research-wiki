# OpenAI – Wikipedia Summary

**Company:** OpenAI is an American AI research organization (San Francisco) consisting of a for-profit public benefit corporation (OpenAI Group PBC) partially controlled by a nonprofit (OpenAI Foundation).

**Founded:** December 8, 2015 (10 years ago)

**Founders (10 total):** Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Ilya Sutskever, Greg Brockman, Trevor Blackwell, Vicki Cheung, Andrej Karpathy, Durk Kingma, John Schulman, Pamela Vagata, Wojciech Zaremba.

**Key Products:** ChatGPT, GPT-5.5, OpenAI Codex, GPT Image, Deep Research, ChatGPT agent, ChatGPT Atlas, ChatGPT Health. Also DALL-E, Sora, Whisper.

**Key People:**
- Sam Altman (CEO)
- Greg Brockman (President / CEO of Applications)
- Bret Taylor (Chairman)
- Sarah Friar (CFO)
- Jakub Pachocki (Chief Scientist)

**Revenue (2025):** US$13.1 billion (est.)
**Net Income (2025):** US$−9 billion
**Valuation (Oct 2025):** $500 billion (most valuable private company)
**Employees (2026):** 4,500

## Founding (2015)

- **Mission:** Ensure AGI "benefits all of humanity"; committed to open collaboration but later restricted access due to competitive/safety concerns.
- **Initial pledges:** $1 billion from Musk, Altman, Brockman, Reid Hoffman, Peter Thiel, Amazon Web Services, Infosys. Actual capital collected by 2021 was $133.2 million.
- **Early work:** Released OpenAI Gym (2016), received Nvidia DGX-1 supercomputer, released Universe platform (Dec 2016).

## Corporate Structure

### Transition from Nonprofit
- **2019:** Shifted to "capped" for-profit structure (OpenAI LP, OpenAI GP LLC, OpenAI Global LLC). Profit capped at 100x any investment.
- **Reason:** Attract venture capital and compensate employees with equity.

### Conversion to Public Benefit Corporation (2025)
- **Oct 28, 2025:** Restructured into Delaware PBC (OpenAI Group PBC), removing profit cap. Nonprofit renamed OpenAI Foundation.
- **Ownership splits:** Foundation 26%, Microsoft 27%, Employees/investors 47%.
- Foundation appoints all PBC board members.
- **Criticism:** Former employees argued restructuring removes governance safeguards. Musk's 2026 lawsuit (lost May 2026) sought to reverse it.

### Partnership with Microsoft
- **Investments:** $1B (2019), $2B (through 2022), $10B (2023) at $29B valuation.
- **2025 restructuring deal:** OpenAI agreed to buy $250B in Azure services; Microsoft ceded right of first refusal. OpenAI will share 20% revenue until AGI (verified by independent panel).
- **April 2026:** Revised agreement — Microsoft no longer exclusive cloud provider; AGI clause dropped.

## Finances

- **2025:** $12B annualized revenue; 20M paid ChatGPT subscribers; $500B valuation after $6.6B share sale
- **2026:** $110B raise (Feb) at $730B valuation; extended to $122B at $852B valuation
- **2026 (planned):** IPO filed June 8, 2026; expected "within next year" (Altman)
- **Long-term:** Projected $600B compute spend by 2030; targeting $200B revenue by 2030; $2.5B ad revenue in 2026, $100B by 2030

**Cash Burn:** $8B operating loss expected in 2025; total $115B through 2029.

## Products & Services

### Key Models/Products
- **GPT Series:** GPT-1 → GPT-2 (2019) → GPT-3 (2020) → GPT-3.5 → GPT-4 → GPT-4o → o1 → o3 → GPT-4.5 → GPT-5.2 → GPT-5.5
- **ChatGPT:** Launched Nov 2022; catalyzed AI boom; 100M users in 2 months (fastest-growing consumer app)
- **DALL-E (2021):** text-to-image model
- **Sora (2024):** text-to-video model
- **OpenAI Codex:** AI agent for coding

### Alignment & Safety
- Initially open-source but later closed for competitive/safety reasons
- **Superalignment project (Jul 2023 – May 2024):** Promised 20% compute, actually allocated 1–2%. Disbanded after co-leaders Sutskever and Leike left
