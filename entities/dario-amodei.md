# Dario Amodei

AI researcher and entrepreneur. Born 1983 in San Francisco. Co-founder and CEO of [[anthropic|Anthropic]] (2021–present). Net worth ~$15.5 billion (Forbes, 2026). Previously VP of Research at OpenAI (2016–2021), researcher at Google and Baidu.

## Education

- Lowell High School (San Francisco), member of 2000 USA Physics Olympiad team
- Stanford University — BS Physics (transferred from Caltech)
- Princeton University — PhD in biophysics (2011), Hertz Foundation Fellow, Hertz Thesis Prize for neural circuit electrophysiology research
- Postdoc at Stanford School of Medicine

## Career

| 2015–2016 | Google | Researcher |

Left OpenAI in 2021 over directional differences. Co-founded Anthropic with sister Daniela Amodei and other former senior OpenAI staff.

As VP of Research at [[openai|OpenAI]] (2016–2021), Amodei oversaw the period during which GPT-3 [(Brown et al., 2020)](raw/papers/2020-05-brown-gpt3/brown2020gpt3.md) — the 175B parameter language model that demonstrated [[in-context-learning|in-context learning]] at scale — and the InstructGPT project (the first large-scale application of [[rlhf|RLHF]] to language model alignment by Ouyang et al., 2022) were conducted.

In November 2023, the OpenAI board approached Amodei to replace Sam Altman as CEO and potentially merge Anthropic with OpenAI — he declined.

## Views on AI

### Benefits — *Machines of Loving Grace* (Oct 2024)

Essay arguing AI could radically improve human welfare across biology, neuroscience, economic development, global peace, work, and the meaning of life. Strongly advocates that **democracies must lead** in AI development. Proposes an "entente" strategy: a coalition of democratic nations uses AI to achieve decisive military/strategic advantage over adversaries.

### Risks — *The Adolescence of Technology* (Jan 2026)

Identifies five major risk categories:

1. **Misalignment** — AI systems developing goals contrary to human intent. Anthropic testing has observed deception, blackmail, and scheming behaviors.
2. **Misuse by individuals/small groups** — especially biological weapons; AI could enable untrained people to create weapons of mass destruction.
3. **Misuse by powerful actors** — authoritarian surveillance, autonomous weapons, mass propaganda. Argues democracies must maintain AI leadership to prevent "global totalitarian dictatorship."
4. **Economic disruption** — massive labor displacement (50% of entry-level white-collar jobs within 1–5 years); wealth concentration exceeding Gilded Age.
5. **Indirect effects & unknowns** — advances in biology altering human lifespan/intelligence; challenges to human purpose when AI exceeds human capability across all domains.

## Pentagon Dispute (Feb–Mar 2026)

Refused to remove contractual ban on Claude usage for mass domestic surveillance or fully autonomous weapons at request of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. Pentagon designated Anthropic a "supply-chain risk"; Trump administration ordered federal agencies to stop using Claude. Multiple amicus briefs filed supporting Anthropic. Federal judge issued temporary injunction (Mar 26, 2026) ruling actions appeared to be "classic First Amendment retaliation."
