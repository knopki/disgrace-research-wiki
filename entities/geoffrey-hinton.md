## Overview

**Geoffrey Everest Hinton** — British-Canadian cognitive psychologist and computer scientist, widely recognized as one of the "godfathers of deep learning" (alongside Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun). His research has shaped foundational techniques from backpropagation to knowledge distillation.

## Key Contributions

- **Backpropagation (1986):** Co-authored the landmark paper with David Rumelhart and Ronald Williams that popularised the backpropagation algorithm, demonstrating a working neural network that learned the XOR function. ([Rumelhart, Hinton & Williams, 1986](concepts/backpropagation.md))

- **Knowledge Distillation (2015):** Formalized the technique of compressing large models into smaller ones via temperature-parameterized soft targets, enabling ensemble compression and model deployment at scale. ([Hinton, Vinyals & Dean, 2015](raw/papers/2015-03-hinton-distillation/hinton2015distill.md))

- **Other notable work:** Dropout (with Nitish Srivastava et al., 2012), capsule networks, unsupervised pre-training for DNNs.
